How each card performed

Impact compares your win rate in games where the card reached your hand with games where it didn't, then sets the deck's typical card to zero, so negative means weaker than average. Cards that only show up in long games can look better or worse than they are, so use it to decide what to test, not as a verdict.

Suggested improvements

Takes your weakest cards and tries owned cards in your colors at a similar mana value. Every candidate gets a quick screen, then the best few replay the full gauntlet so the result can be compared with your current list.
